9Bit

< Core Concept

You play as a single "bit" of computer memory navigating through the web to reach a target IP address before the server times out.

< Mechanics

Toggle between 2 phases:

Phase 0 (Circle)

Moves fluidly, rolls down slopes, and retains momentum but cannot jump. Great for speed.

Phase 1 (Square)

Heavy and precise. Stops instantly, jumps, grips edges, and won't slide off narrow platforms.

< Enemies

Virus

Roams the circuit waiting to catch a bit and corrupt it.

Trojan Horse

Camouflages as a server to bait you in and corrupt your bit.

Hacker

Roams the level, approaches you, and fires freezing projectiles.

Malware Blaster

Cannon obstacle firing projectiles upon detecting the Bit.

< Servers

  • Phase Matching: Hit the server in the correct binary phase (0 or 1) to activate it.
  • Decryption Chains: Activate all servers in the correct order to decrypt the level's hidden word.
  • Honeypots: Deploy these as decoys to lure out Trojan Horses and Viruses from your path.

< Future Prospects

  • Procedural Level Generation: Dynamically generated circuit board tracks for infinite replayability.
  • Dynamic Enemy Placement: Algorithmic spawning of Hackers and Viruses to adapt to player skill.
